## Ops dashboard triage

User role: Support lead trying to clear stuck task executions before standup.
Initial prompt: The task execution dashboard is noisy. Make it easier to scan without turning it into a marketing page.
Question type: recommended choice

Questions and simulated answers:
1. [target user] Who is the primary operator for the dense dashboard?
   user: Support leads who triage failed and stale executions during daily ops.
2. [primary workflow] Which workflow should the first pass optimize?
   user: Spot failed or stale runs, compare owner/status/age, then jump into the run.
3. [visual evidence] What proof should count before implementation starts?
   user: Before/after screenshots at desktop and mobile widths plus one focused Playwright check.

Resulting executable plan:
```text
Context: Support lead trying to clear stuck task executions before standup.
Target Product Contract: Support leads can identify stale or failed runs within 10 seconds in the screenshot review.
Scope In: dashboard density and hierarchy, existing design primitives, desktop and mobile screenshot evidence
Scope Out: new dashboard information architecture, decorative landing-page style redesign
Decisions: target user=Support leads who triage failed and stale executions during daily ops.; primary workflow=Spot failed or stale runs, compare owner/status/age, then jump into the run.; visual evidence=Before/after screenshots at desktop and mobile widths plus one focused Playwright check.
Tool Guidance: read_repo_instructions, git_status, ripgrep, read_files, browser_qa, targeted_tests, no_mutation_in_plan_mode
Success Metric: Support leads can identify stale or failed runs within 10 seconds in the screenshot review.
Counter-Metric: No table row, toolbar, or action text wraps or overlaps at mobile width.
Evidence Artifact: screenshots
Discoverable Unknowns: existing design primitives, current viewport behavior
Confidence Gaps: design primitive ownership, viewport evidence
Planning Summary Review: Plan is approved around support-lead triage, screenshot proof, and no broad redesign.
```
